# Tyler: Call Me If You Get Lost license

`callmeifyougetlostid.png` is the front face artwork. It is used as supplied, including its printed labels, handwritten issue date, and photo box. The rear face is the editable cream placeholder in `back.svg`.

Field positions are percentages in `src/templates/tyler-call-me-if-you-get-lost/config.ts`. Adjust the configured rectangles in dev mode with **outline field boxes** enabled. Keep the front artwork in landscape orientation at the standard 85.6 × 54 mm card proportion.

The handwriting and typewriter fonts are loaded locally through `@fontsource` packages. Photos, typed signatures, drawn signatures, and generated QR codes are processed in the browser.
